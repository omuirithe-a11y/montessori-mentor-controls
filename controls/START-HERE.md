## Design figures (approved 13 Oct 2026; all spaces letters to letters)
Page
1. Text column 1220 wide.
2. Beige line between sections: 30 above, 30 below. A beige line before and after every quotation, 30 from the letters.
3. Navy footer band 40 above and below; the footer quotation and its attribution 18px.
4. Hero and top pictures 1600 wide.
5. Text justified on computers (641px and wider), no hyphenation; phones left-aligned. Quotations, captions, tab lines and lists are not justified.
6. The "Table of contents" title stands alone on its own line above the columns.

Spaces
7. Between paragraphs 20.
8. Under a heading 20. Above a heading 30, including a heading directly under another heading.
9. Bold sub-heading: 30 above, 12 to its text.
10. List lines 15 apart; 30 above and below a list; a one-line list that finishes its sentence 14 above.
11. Quotations 18px, lines 23 apart; 25 into and out of a quotation; first line and attribution indented one tab (48).
12. Rows of photos 30 above and below.

Photos
13. Photos in a grid or beside text 120 high, centred on their text; rows stay 168; a single photo beside a paragraph takes the paragraph's height, up to 168. On phones photos stand one under another, centred.
14. Bulleted lists indented one tab (48) on computers; numbered lists at the margin; phones no indent.

Menu (approved 13 Oct 2026)
15. Category titles (for example The Mathematics Curriculum) are blue, and nothing in the menu is bold.
16. Leading entries (the first line of a group when it is the section's own page, for example Administration in the Play, Administration, Parents and Resources panels) are blue too.
17. On phones the titles are the light blue #8FC6FF.

Two kinds of page, two templates. Essay pages: the live page https://montessorimentor.co.uk/the-practical-life-curriculum-2/ is the template. Resources pages: the live page https://montessorimentor.co.uk/practical-life-resources/ is the template.
Temporary: the two live pages are the templates until the numbers-only template is made (planned 14 Oct 2026).

Lasting preferences (colours, dashes, photos, Hostinger, links, menu, footer) are kept in the project's memory notes.

## Rules
1. Nothing goes live or gets uploaded without your "go".
2. One page at a time.
3. I build what you asked, send a preview, and stop.
4. If an instruction is unclear, I ask one short question that names what is missing and gives options.
5. I don't re-ask anything you have already settled.
6. Page text comes from your Word documents word for word; I never write wording for your pages. Exception: correct all typos.
7. I only say something is checked or done if I tell you what I looked at.
8. Everything is in plain English. I never ask you to run commands. Links go on their own line. Whenever I tell you to go somewhere (a settings page, a website, a file), I give the full link on its own line first, then the steps.
9. Faces: blurring only where you ask, and never for the 11 September documents.
10. If something is blocked or refused, I stop and tell you; I don't find another way.
11. If you ask a question, I answer it and change nothing.
12. Always check by asking which are headings and which are sub headings.
13. Your originals (Word files, PDFs, photos) are never edited; I work on copies.
