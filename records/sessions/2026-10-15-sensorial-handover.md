## How the page works (for any further change)
- One HTML file. A layout script sets photo sizes and rows. Extra scripts (ids: beige-line-gaps, touch-table-gaps-js, edits-15-oct-b-js, centre-extension-photos, mm-edits-14-oct-js) set spacing and centring from the real letter edges (ink), switch off at 640 px and below, and re-run after each layout and after fonts load.
